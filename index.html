The problem

Paper on site. Spreadsheets in the office. One HR person in the middle.

Most HR software assumes every employee has a phone, an email address and a login. On a depot, a mine site or a workshop floor, many don't. So the work lands on HR: leave forms in a drawer, contracts copied from last year's Word file, warnings filed who-knows-where, and timesheets retyped line by line.

This system was built inside exactly that kind of company. It keeps the paper your people trust and takes the retyping off your desk.

What it does

Everything HR touches, in one place.

One login for your HR team. Every employee's full history, from appointment to exit, a click away.

01

Paper timesheets, read by AI

Scan the signed sheet. The system drafts the record, the employee checks a printout, and only a signed confirmation becomes final. Nothing reaches payroll unchecked.

02

Employee records

Every person, contract, site and company in one register, with your own employee numbering. Works across several companies in a group.

03

Leave

Annual, sick and other leave captured from paper forms, with balances that update themselves.

04

Contracts

Generate employment contracts from your own templates as Word files. Renewal reminders, with the new term matched to the last one.

05

Discipline & IR

Counselling, warnings and hearing outcomes on each employee's file, so you can see the full history before you act.

06

Compliance reports

Employment equity / Affirmative Action reporting from data you already hold, instead of a week of spreadsheets.

07

Training & levy claims

Track training and build the evidence for training-levy grant claims, so money you're owed doesn't go unclaimed.

08

Dashboards

Headcount, turnover, movement, contract renewals, leave and performance. Ready for your monthly management meeting.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do our employees need phones or email?

No. The system is run by your HR team. Employees keep using paper forms and signed timesheets, and the system turns them into records.

Can AI change someone's pay by mistake?

No. AI only makes a draft from the scanned timesheet. The employee checks a printout and signs it, and only that signed confirmation becomes final data.

Can you load our existing records?

Yes. Loading your current employees, leave balances and contract details from spreadsheets or exports is part of set-up.

Does it work outside Namibia?

Yes. Leave rules, contract wording and compliance reports are configured for your country during set-up, and the system works in your currency.

Where is our data kept?

In a secure cloud database that belongs to your company alone. Each client gets a separate system, and you can ask for a full export at any time.

Are we locked into a contract?

No. It runs month to month. If it stops earning its keep, you can stop.
